## 3-min version (additive structure)

1. **Problem** — 香港生活服務碎片化，沒人幫用戶跑完整件 task
2. **Solution** — Cantonese-first AI concierge; transparency + human accountability
3. **Why now** — WhatsApp 習慣 + FPS 普及 + AI 工具成熟
4. **Market** — SAM HK$1.67bn (task-based, no leakage)
5. **Traction** — 100 → 1,000 → 5,000 月完成 run-rate trajectory
6. **Model** — 13% take + coordination fee; 5–7x revenue multiple by Y3
7. **Team** — Founder profile + agent team + advisor list
8. **Ask** — HK$12–15m pre-seed/seed, 18mo runway
9. **Use of funds** — 28% eng, 29% team, 15% ops, 15% GTM, 5% legal, 8% ops cash
10. **Vision** — Trusted Agent for HK; expand category then city
