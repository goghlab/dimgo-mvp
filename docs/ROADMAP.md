## 🎯 North-star

> **成功完成嘅多步任務數量**
>
> (Not downloads, not chat messages, not MAU.)

---

## 📊 Phase Overview

| Phase | Window | Goal | Exit criteria |
|-------|--------|------|---------------|
| **0 — Concierge 驗證** | W1–W8 | 用最少嘅產品成本（landing + WhatsApp + 真人調度）證明「用戶真係願意交俾 DimGo 完成任務」 | 100 宗完成 · 10 supplier · CSAT ≥ 4.3 · 50 waitlist |
| **1 — 受控 MVP** | M3–M6 | Build the smallest product that can scale the Phase 0 wins | 1,000 累計 · 30–50 服務商 · 廣東話 Concierge live · CSAT ≥ 4.3 |
| **2 — 交易編排** | M7–M12 | Bundle power + SME 月結 + first official API partner | 5,000+ 月 run-rate · 75 SME · 1 個正式合作 |
| **3 — Trusted Agent** | M13–M24 | From "app users open for one task" to "the first thing they open when they have a task" | 月 GMV ≥ HK$2m · 貢獻毛利為正 · 30 日重複達標 |

**No phase-jumping without meeting the prior phase's exit criteria.**
